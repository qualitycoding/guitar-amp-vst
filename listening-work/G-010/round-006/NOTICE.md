# Round 6 listener bundle: licence and attribution

The WAV files in `listener-bundle/` (and `listener-bundle.zip`) are **not** covered by this repository's Apache-2.0
licence. Each pair holds one clip derived from a real-amp recording licensed
[CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) and one clip rendered by GrimAmp from the DI of the same
performance. Use for **non-commercial purposes only**.

Sources (ToneTwist AFx Dataset, Comunità et al.):

- Fender Blues Junior: doi:10.5281/zenodo.10797977 (GuitarML data, Keith Bloemer), CC BY-NC 4.0
- Mesa/Boogie Mark V, Extreme channel: doi:10.5281/zenodo.10796864 (Schmitz and Embrechts data), CC BY-NC 4.0

Changes made: a 12 s window cut from each recording, converted to mono 44.1 kHz, a 1 s onset excerpt taken, 10 ms
fades, level matched to -26 dBFS RMS, 24-bit WAV. Which side of each pair is the recording is withheld; the answer
key is not in the repository.

Exception to `DEVIATIONS.md` D-001ac (owner instruction, 2026-11-05): only this listener bundle is committed. The
full-length excerpts, DIs and GrimAmp renders stay local.
